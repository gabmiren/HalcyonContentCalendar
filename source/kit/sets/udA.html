Why it happens
The first candle after news can lie
1

Orders thin out in the seconds around a release, so price jumps further on less.

2

Stops sit on both sides, and a fast spike can reach them before the real move starts.

3

Some traders wait for the first 5-minute candle to close before they decide anything.

What you control
That spike was 38% of a drawdown
EXAMPLE
38 points on 1 NQ100 points = $2,000
One 5-minute candle, on one contract. Knowing your drawdown in points tells you how many of those you can sit through.
